CliniQ

v1.0 · Smart Dental Queue System

CliniQ is a modern dental clinic queue management system designed to eliminate waiting room chaos. Patients can book appointments, track their queue position, and view their visit history — all from their phone. Clinic staff get a real-time dashboard with queue management, revenue tracking, and dentist availability controls.
